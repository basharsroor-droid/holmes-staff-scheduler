# App Review response — Guideline 3.2 (Business)

Use the English response below in **App Store Connect → App Review → Reply to App Review**.

## Ready-to-send response

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for your review. ShiftPilot is a public, multi-tenant workforce scheduling service and is not restricted to one company, one organization, or a preselected group of companies.

1. **Is the app restricted to users who are part of a single company or organization?**

   No. ShiftPilot serves multiple independent businesses. Each business has a separate workspace and its own role-based access. The app is not affiliated with, limited to, or distributed for one specific organization.

2. **Is the app designed for use by a limited or specific group of companies or organizations?**

   No. Any eligible business can register for ShiftPilot through our public signup flow at https://www.shiftpilothq.com/onboarding. No sales approval, private allowlist, or affiliation with a specific organization is required. After a business owner creates a workspace, they can invite their own managers and employees.

3. **What features are intended for use by the general public?**

   Any business owner can discover the app, open the public business-registration link shown on the app's login screen, create a workspace, select a plan, and start a 30-day trial without an invitation or credit card. The service supports employee availability, shift scheduling, schedule publication, shift-swap requests, notifications, departments, and role-based access for any registered customer organization.

4. **How do users obtain an account?**

   A business owner self-registers through the public signup flow linked directly from the app. Registration is available to any business and requires email verification. Managers and employees receive an invitation from their own business administrator and then activate their individual accounts. Existing users sign in through the app's login screen.

5. **Is there any paid content in the app and, if so, who pays for it?**

   ShiftPilot is a business SaaS service. The business owner or organization is responsible for the business subscription; individual employees do not pay. New organizations start with a 30-day trial without a credit card. The iOS app does not sell digital media, consumer content, virtual currency, or individual in-app features.

We have also updated the iOS login screen to state clearly that ShiftPilot is open to employees, managers, and business owners from any registered organization. A prominent **"Create a new business"** button opens the public registration flow on the ShiftPilot website.

Please reconsider the app for public App Store distribution. We are happy to provide any additional information needed.

Best regards,  
ShiftPilot Team
